BitMaker: turn off the board LEDs

Block: bitmaker_leds_eteindre

What is it for?

Turns off the 4 RGB LEDs of the BitMaker V2 board.

How to use it?

Put it after showing something, for example with the ‘wait’ block to make the LEDs blink.

Good to know

  • Only the board LEDs turn off: the LED matrix of the micro:bit does not change.

Example

The board LEDs blink in yellow, on for half a second, off for half a second.
