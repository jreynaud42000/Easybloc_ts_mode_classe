name the axes: X … Y …

Block: graph_axes

What is it for?

Writes the name of each axis of the graph, with its unit: ‘Time (s)’ under the horizontal axis (X), ‘Temperature (°C)’ along the vertical axis (Y).

How to use it?

Put this block only once, in ‘on start’. Click in the boxes to type your names.

A scientific graph always has named axes, with the unit in brackets.

Good to know

  • The names of the axes stay after the ‘clear the graph’ block.

Example

The axes are named on start, then the light measured by the LED matrix is plotted every second.
