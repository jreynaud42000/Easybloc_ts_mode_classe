MP3 player on … : play mode …

Block: grove_mp3_mode

What is it for?

Chooses what the Grove MP3 player (v3.0) does at the end of a track: stop (once), repeat the track, repeat all (all the tracks in a loop) or play the tracks in random order (shuffle).

How to use it?

Put it in ‘on start’, before playing a track.

Good to know

  • ‘repeat all’ is handy for background music; ‘repeat the track’ for an alarm sound that loops.

Example

On start, the player switches to ‘repeat all’, then plays track 1: all the tracks follow one another endlessly.
