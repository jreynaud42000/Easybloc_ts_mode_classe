BitMaker: all the board LEDs in …

Block: bitmaker_leds

What is it for?

Lights the 4 RGB LEDs of the BitMaker V2 board in the same colour.

How to use it?

Choose the colour in the block's list.

Good to know

  • The LEDs of the board are linked to pin P13.

Example

The board LEDs light up in green when button A is pressed, in red with button B.
