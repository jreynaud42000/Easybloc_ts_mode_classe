BitMaker: LED no. … of the board in …

Block: bitmaker_led

What is it for?

Lights one of the 4 RGB LEDs soldered on the BitMaker V2 board, in the chosen colour.

How to use it?

Choose the number of the LED (1 to 4) and the colour. The colour black turns the LED off.

Good to know

  • The LEDs of the board are linked to pin P13: do not plug any other module into it.
  • The other LEDs keep their colour.

Example

LEDs 1 to 4 light up in red one after the other, then they all turn off, forever.
